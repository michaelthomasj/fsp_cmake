TF-M on Renesas RA — RA6E1 → RA6M5 → RA8x2 (single-core), FSP 6.6, GNUARM + IAR

Descoped 2026-09-14 — dual-core

The previous revision made RA8x2 dual-core the primary goal, with an entire phase dedicated to binding FSP inter-core communication to TF-M's tfm_hal_multi_core_* / platform_mailbox, an NS mailbox agent, and PSA call marshaling across cores. It carried the two highest risks in the register — the mailbox integration ("no RA reference exists") and the unexamined core-topology question. All of it is removed; RA8x2 is a single-core part for this project. Retained into P6: the base port, TrustZone config, BSP, and the NS + IAR + crypto-app work.
Buffer. Dates are targets, not commitments — an overrun shifts subsequent dates rather than cutting scope. The RA8x2 leg holds Dec 21 – Jan 2 as contingency. Everything is sequential under one engineer.
